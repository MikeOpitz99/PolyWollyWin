# Acknowledgements

PolyWollyWin was developed through direct testing of the ASUS ROG Strix Flare II Animate and live USBPcap traffic analysis.

The project was also informed by ideas, documentation, and prior open-source work from the following projects.

## Polylux

- Project: https://github.com/vladulus/polylux
- Author: vladulus

Polylux demonstrates direct control of several ASUS ROG hardware devices without requiring the full Armoury Crate stack.

## asusctl and rog-anime

- Project: https://gitlab.com/asus-linux/asusctl
- Authors: flukejones and contributors
- Licence: Mozilla Public License 2.0

The asusctl project contains extensive open-source research into ASUS ROG hardware and AniMe Matrix devices on Linux.

## G-Helper

- Project: https://github.com/seerge/g-helper
- Licence: MIT

G-Helper demonstrates a lightweight approach to controlling ASUS hardware on Windows.

## Protocol confirmation

The HID wire format currently used by PolyWollyWin for the ROG Strix Flare II Animate, `VID 0x0B05`, `PID 0x19FC`, was independently confirmed from live USBPcap captures made while the official software controlled the keyboard.
